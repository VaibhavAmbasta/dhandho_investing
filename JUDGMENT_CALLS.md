# Accounting and data judgment calls

Each item is a choice that changes numbers. Every one is either a value in
`config.yaml` or flagged here so you can overrule it.

## Phase 1: data layer

| # | Call | Where | Why / risk |
|---|------|-------|-----------|
| 1 | **Fiscal-year label = calendar year of the period end**, except a 52/53-week year ending in the first 7 days of January is labelled the prior year. | `ingest.fiscal_year_january_rollback_days` | Matches AAPL/MSFT/COST/AZO naming. **Mismatches some Jan-FYE retailers' own naming** (Home Depot calls the year ending Jan-2024 "fiscal 2023"; we call it 2024). Deterministic, but not the filer's own label. |
| 2 | **Canonical value = best-ranked strategy, then the latest filing within it.** Every vintage is kept in `concept_vintages` with its accession and filing date; `db.values_as_of()` applies the same rule as of a date. Values that other tags reported differently go into `notes`. `restated` means the *same* tag changed value across filings. | `facts.compute_concept` | Ranking first exists because of a bug found in real data: AAPL's later cash-flow statements repeat old opening cash under the restricted-cash tag, and "latest filing wins" let that displace balance-sheet cash for FY2016-2023. Downside: a lower-ranked tag carrying a genuine restatement (e.g. full-retrospective ASC 606) loses to the original higher-ranked tag. Latest values are **not what an investor saw at the time**, so backtests must use `values_as_of`. |
| 3 | **Annual = duration of 340-390 days.** 10-KT transition periods and quarterly figures inside 10-Ks are dropped. | `ingest.annual_duration_days` | A fiscal-year-end change leaves a gap year (reported in the fiscal-period notes). |
| 4 | **Fiscal years = each 10-K's main period + comparative years reported in ≥ 2 filings.** Pre-XBRL comparative years (e.g. 2007-2008) are included. | `facts.detect_fiscal_periods` | Those years have income-statement data but few balance-sheet values. Flagged as warnings. |
| 5 | **Sums: missing optional components count as zero.** E.g. debt = LT debt non-current (required) + current portion + commercial paper + short-term borrowings (optional). | `concepts.*.strategies` | A company that tags its current portion under a tag we don't list gets **understated debt with no error**. The verify printout shows every component so you can catch this. |
| 6 | **Debt excludes finance leases. Commercial paper is added only when `ShortTermBorrowings` is not reported**, because the us-gaap short-term borrowings total includes CP. | `concepts.total_debt` (`contained_in`) | Found in real data: KO 2021 counted $2.46B of CP twice (in its $3.31B loans and notes payable) until this rule was added. Remaining error: when a filer tags CP but not the short-term total, any non-CP short-term debt is missed (KO 2022 is understated by about $0.2B). The `LongTermDebtAndCapitalLeaseObligations` fallback (KO 2023+) *includes* finance leases, which makes it slightly inconsistent with earlier years. |
| 7 | **No zero-filling.** Missing goodwill, intangibles, debt or SBC is stored as NULL plus a reason ("company may have none"), never 0. | `concepts.*.absent_note` | Phases 2-3 must choose explicitly when NULL means 0. A silent zero would hide real data gaps. |
| 8 | **Operating income has no fallback** to pre-tax income. | `concepts.operating_income` | Banks and insurers get NULL. Mixing EBIT with EBT would corrupt margins and ROIC. |
| 9 | **Revenue: `Revenues` tried before the ASC 606 contract-revenue tag.** | `concepts.revenue` | `Revenues` is total revenue. `RevenueFromContractWithCustomer...` excludes lease, interest and other non-contract revenue. Order affects when tag switches show up. |
| 10 | **Net income = attributable to parent** (`NetIncomeLoss`). `ProfitLoss` (includes NCI) is the last fallback. **Equity**'s fallback also includes NCI. | `concepts.net_income`, `equity` | Flagged in the tag log whenever the fallback is used. |
| 11 | **Cash fallback includes restricted cash.** Marketable securities are **separate** concepts (`short_term_investments`, `long_term_investments`), which I added beyond your list. | `concepts.cash` and the extras | Netting only cash against debt would wrongly put AAPL in heavy net debt. Whether *long-term* securities count as cash is a Phase 2 decision. |
| 12 | **Capex = PP&E purchases only.** Capitalized software, acquisitions and intangible purchases are excluded. | `concepts.capex` | Overstates owner FCF for companies that capitalize a lot of software, and for serial acquirers (e.g. roll-ups). |
| 13 | **SBC = pre-tax cash-flow add-back.** | `concepts.stock_based_comp` | Phase 2 subtracts pre-tax SBC, which ignores the tax shield. That is conservative. |
| 14 | **Receivables = trade receivables only.** | `concepts.receivables` | Excludes AAPL's vendor non-trade receivables, which is correct for DSO. |
| 15 | **Operating leases are "not applicable" for periods ending before 2019-12-15** (pre-ASC 842). A **zero** reported before that date is also treated as not applicable. | `concepts.operating_lease_liabilities` | Early adopters keep real values (MSFT from FY2017). Zero placeholders in pre-adoption comparative columns (MSFT FY2016, CPRT FY2019) are dropped instead of being read as "no lease debt". Pre-842 lease debt is **not** reconstructed from disclosure notes, so lease-adjusted net debt is not comparable across 2019. |
| 16 | **Diluted shares = weighted-average diluted, as reported. Not split-adjusted.** | `concepts.diluted_shares` | XBRL comparatives are only split-adjusted in filings made after the split. Older years stay pre-split (AAPL 2020 4:1, CPRT, ORLY 2025 15:1). The coverage report flags jumps over 1.6x. **Must be fixed before Phase 2 dilution math.** |
| 17 | **Conflicting duplicates in one filing:** keep the fact whose duration is closest to 364 days, otherwise the first one. A note is stored with the value. | `facts._pick_one` | Rare; visible in the `notes` column and in the verify output. |
| 18 | **Only facts without dimensions are used** (companyfacts excludes dimensional facts). | SEC API | Segment and consolidated-subsidiary breakdowns are unavailable from this endpoint. |
| 19 | **Financials (JPM, BRK-B, MKL) go through the same industrial mapping.** | `tickers.txt` | Revenue, operating income, debt, capex and DSO are not meaningful for a bank or insurer. Expect gaps and misleading values. They are included on purpose to show where the screener breaks. |
| 20 | **Filer XBRL errors are flagged, not corrected.** | `coverage` warnings | CPRT FY2012 diluted shares are tagged as 131,428 (thousands tagged as units, about 1000x too small). The coverage report flags year-over-year share jumps over 100x as scale errors, but the stored value stays wrong. Phase 2 must exclude or override it. |
| 21 | **Goodwill or intangibles that are no longer reported separately stay NULL.** | `concepts.goodwill` | AAPL has not broken out goodwill since FY2018. NULL is correct, but a Phase 3 goodwill/assets ratio cannot be computed for those years. Treating it as 0 would be wrong. |

## Phase 2: reverse DCF

| # | Call | Where | Why / risk |
|---|------|-------|-----------|
| 22 | **Financials are excluded** by SIC code 6000-6799 (from the SEC submissions API). JPM, BRK-B and MKL stay in the database but get no valuation. | `screen.exclude_sic_ranges` | Owner FCF, debt and EV are not meaningful for banks and insurers. Also excludes REITs (SIC 6798), which would need FFO instead. |
| 23 | **Splits are inferred from SEC data only**: the ratio of the same fiscal year's diluted shares in consecutive 10-Ks. Clean split ratios and exact 1000x scale errors are applied; anything else is reported and NOT applied. | `shares.py`, `ingest.split_ratio_tolerance` | Recovers AAPL 7:1 and 4:1, CPRT 2:1 and 4:1, ORLY 15:1, KO 2:1, and repairs CPRT's FY2014 filing tagged in thousands. Misses a split if no later 10-K restated an overlapping year (e.g. a split after the latest 10-K). |
| 24 | **Dilution is NOT modelled by default** (`model_dilution: false`). Owner FCF already deducts SBC, and buybacks are paid out of it, so also shrinking or growing the share count would charge SBC twice and credit buybacks twice. The historical dilution rate is still reported, and setting `model_dilution: true` restores the per-share model you specified. | `reverse_dcf.model_dilution` | Turned off because the double count was large: AZO's implied growth moved 7pp from this choice alone. Not captured: dilution from stock issued for acquisitions. |
| 25 | **Starting owner FCF = normalized**: the median owner-FCF margin of the last 5 years × latest revenue. `latest` and `avg3` are reported alongside. | `reverse_dcf.base_fcf`, `normalization` | Ignores one-off years (KO FY2024-25: latest $5.0B → normalized $9.9B) and keeps the company's current size. **Overstates when margins are in structural decline**: MSFT's owner-FCF margin fell from 27% (FY2022) to 9% (FY2026) as AI capex grew; normalized uses the 21% median. If that capex intensity is permanent, MSFT's base is too high and its implied growth too low. |
| 26 | **Long-term marketable debt securities count as cash** in net debt; operating leases count as debt. | `reverse_dcf.net_debt` | AAPL's net debt goes from +$56B to -$21B. Equity-method stakes and `LongTermInvestments` are deliberately not mapped, so they never count as cash. No haircut for repatriation tax or operating cash needs. |
| 27 | **A NULL net-debt component is treated as 0 and listed in `assumptions`.** A NULL SBC is 0 only if the company never reported SBC in any year; otherwise owner FCF is NULL for that year. | `valuation.py` | A missing cash value inflates net debt; a missing debt value deflates it. Every case is stated per ticker. |
| 28 | **Market cap = price x cover-page shares outstanding** (`dei:EntityCommonStockSharesOutstanding`, from the latest 10-K or 10-Q). This is a basic count, not diluted; option/RSU dilution enters only through the dilution rate. | `valuation.py` | A warning fires if the count is over 120 days old (KO) or differs from diluted shares by more than 15% (a split after the cover date, or multiple share classes). |
| 29 | **Timing is simplified**: the balance sheet and base FCF are from the last fiscal year end (up to ~12 months old) while the price is today's. Cash flows are discounted at year ends from today, with no stub period and no mid-year convention. | `dcf.py` | Biased towards a slightly higher implied growth for companies whose fiscal year ended long ago. |
| 30 | **Terminal value = multiple x year-10 owner FCF** (not a perpetuity-growth formula). | `reverse_dcf.terminal_multiple` | 15x at a 10% discount equals a perpetuity growth of about 3.2%. |
| 31 | **Historical owner-FCF CAGR uses normalized endpoints** (the same median-margin × revenue method as the starting FCF, at both ends of a 10-year span). Revenue CAGR still uses 3-year trailing means. The previous 3-year-mean version is reported as `hist_fcf_cagr_mean`. If data is missing, the span shrinks (minimum 5 years). | `reverse_dcf.history.fcf_endpoints` | History and base are now compared like for like. KO: −2.1% (mean, dragged down by one-offs) → +3.1% (normalized). Lags a genuine margin shift by about 2-3 years. |
| 32 | **Rank = gap between implied and historical owner-FCF CAGR, most negative first.** | `dcf_report.rank` | Rewards cheapness relative to the past, not the quality of the past; Phases 3-4 must supply the quality check. |
| 33 | **Held-to-maturity securities count as cash only when no other securities line is reported.** `OtherShortTermInvestments` is summed with current marketable securities. | `concepts.held_to_maturity_securities`, `concepts.short_term_investments`, `reverse_dcf.net_debt.include_held_to_maturity` | Found in real data: CPRT's $2.58B of HTM Treasuries was missing (net cash $1.8B → $4.4B), and KO's $3.6B of short-term investments was missing. COST's $0.34B HTM is already inside its $1.12B short-term investments line, so it is skipped and the skip is listed in `assumptions`. A company whose HTM is a *separate* line next to marketable securities will be understated. MSFT's $36B `LongTermInvestments` (which includes equity stakes) remains excluded on purpose. |
| 34 | **Assets taken on through finance leases count as capex** (`RightOfUseAssetObtainedInExchangeForFinanceLeaseLiability`, or before ASC 842 `CapitalLeaseObligationsIncurred`). | `reverse_dcf.capex.include_finance_lease_additions` | A finance lease is a purchase paid in instalments; the repayments appear under financing, so ordinary FCF never sees them. MSFT FY2026: +$24.6B of capex (latest owner FCF $54.6B → $30.0B; 10-year FCF growth 10.1% → 6.0% on mean endpoints). The cost is counted when the asset is taken on, not as it is paid for. |
| 35 | **Upkeep capex ≈ PP&E depreciation** (+ finance-lease asset amortization), capped at total capex. If only total D&A is tagged, intangible amortization is subtracted when reported; otherwise D&A is used and the assumption is listed. Used **only** for earning power value (owner earnings after upkeep capex, normalized, ÷ discount rate), never in the growth solve. | `concepts.depreciation*`, `reverse_dcf.earning_power` | Depreciation is at historical cost, so in inflationary periods it **understates** real upkeep (overstating earning power). Treating growth capex as optional in a no-growth value is the Greenwald earning-power method; mixing it into the growth solve would treat growth as free. `growth_premium` = 1 − EPV / EV, the share of the price that is a bet on growth. |
